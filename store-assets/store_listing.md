Welcome to Block Blast Star: Jewel Match – the ultimate addictive block puzzle and brain training game!

Looking for a relaxing yet stimulating puzzle adventure? Block Blast Star combines the classic 8x8 grid block puzzle mechanics with strategic Sudoku placement, dazzling jewel explosions, rhythmic combo sound effects, and exciting power boosters!

Whether you want to unwind after a long day or keep your mind sharp and active, Block Blast Star is your perfect daily companion. Best of all, it's 100% free and playable offline anytime, anywhere – no Wi-Fi or internet connection required!

Earn boosters naturally through daily gameplay, milestone trophies, or free rewards.
