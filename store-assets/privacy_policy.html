Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 (v1.0.1)

This Privacy Policy describes how Brain Bloom (Block Blast Star) ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our application on Android, iOS, iPadOS, or web browsers.

Key Takeaway: Brain Bloom (Block Blast Star) is a standalone, offline-first puzzle game. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or identifying information to external servers or third parties.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute privacy for our players. Our application:

  • Does NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Does NOT access your contact list, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • Does NOT track your precise or approximate GPS location.
  • Does NOT track user behavior across other apps or websites.

2. Local Device Storage (Offline Data)

To provide a seamless gaming experience, the App stores gameplay progress strictly locally on your device's internal storage sandbox (via HTML5 LocalStorage). This includes:

  • Your highest score, active game state (for Resume Game), combo records, and lines cleared statistics.
  • In-game virtual coin balances, free trial counters (Undo/Hint), and booster inventory counts (Rotate, Shuffle, Hammer).
  • Unlocked achievements, trophy quests, and Daily Puzzle calendar completion streaks.
  • Visual theme preferences (Neon, Wood, Candy), comfort mode settings, sound effect, and haptic vibration preferences.

This data remains strictly on your personal device and is never transmitted to us or any remote server. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage through your device settings will reset this local data.

3. Permissions

Brain Bloom (Block Blast Star) requests minimal device permissions necessary solely for basic rendering and tactile haptic feedback (VIBRATE). We do not request any sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, or external storage read/write.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

This version of the application does not integrate external behavioral tracking SDKs, third-party analytics frameworks, or automated advertising networks. No user profiles are created or shared.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is family-friendly and safe for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy standards.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
